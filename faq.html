Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers! Find everything you need to know about renting cameras and gadgets from us.

How do I book a camera or gadget?

Booking is easy! Follow these simple steps:

  1. Check availability on our Available Cameras page
  2. Contact us through Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp
  3. Provide rental dates and documents (ID)
  4. Pay rental fee plus security deposit
  5. Pick up at our location or opt for delivery

After rental:

  1. Use equipment responsibly
  2. Return on agreed date
  3. Get deposit refund

Booking tips:

  1. Book 2-3 days in advance
  2. Check equipment condition on pickup
  3. Keep all accessories safe

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, we require a refundable security deposit for all rentals. The deposit amount varies depending on the gadget's value (typically ₱3,000-₱10,000). The full deposit is refunded immediately upon return of the equipment in good condition.

We accept GCash, bank transfer, or cash for deposits.

What happens if the equipment gets damaged?

We understand that accidents happen. Minor wear and tear is expected and acceptable. However, if there's significant damage, the repair cost will be deducted from your security deposit. If the damage exceeds the deposit amount, you'll be responsible for the additional costs.

Can I extend my rental period?

Yes! You can extend your rental period subject to availability. Contact us before your return date to check if the gadget is available for extension. Extended rental rates will apply, and you'll need to pay the additional rental fee.

Quick tip: Press Ctrl + E to access extension form. Available in English, Tagalog, and 日本語にほんご (Japanese). We'll update your rental agreement accordingly.

Do you offer delivery and pickup services?

Yes, we offer delivery and pickup services within Metro Manila for an additional fee. The delivery fee depends on your location (typically ₱200-₱500). For rentals of 11 days or more, delivery may be complimentary within select areas. You can also pick up and return the gadget at our location for free.

What documents do I need to rent?

You'll need to provide: (1) One valid government-issued ID (driver's license, passport, UMID, SSS, etc.), (2) Filled-out rental agreement form, and (3) Payment for rental fee and security deposit. For corporate/business rentals, we may require additional documents like company ID or business permit.

What's included in every rental?

Every gadget rental includes: battery and charger, memory card (16GB-64GB depending on device), protective bag or case, protective cover or screen protector, and accessories like tripod, selfie stick, or mounts (depending on the gadget). We also provide a free tutorial on how to use the equipment!

Do you provide tutorials on how to use the cameras?

Yes! We provide free basic tutorials for all our equipment. Our staff will show you how to operate the camera, change settings, and use the included accessories. For more advanced techniques or professional training, we can arrange extended sessions with our photography partners.

What if the gadget I want is not available?

If your preferred gadget is unavailable, we can suggest similar alternatives that meet your needs. You can also join our waitlist, and we'll notify you as soon as it becomes available. We update our inventory regularly and may have new units coming in soon.

Can I rent multiple gadgets at once?

Absolutely! You can rent multiple gadgets for the same period. We offer package deals for multiple items, which can save you money. Just let us know what equipment you need, and we'll create a customized rental package for you.

Do you rent to tourists or non-residents?

Yes, we welcome tourists and non-residents! You'll need to provide a valid passport and may need to pay a higher security deposit. We can also arrange hotel delivery and pickup for your convenience. Contact us in advance to arrange your rental.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods: GCash, PayMaya, bank transfer, and cash. For online bookings, we prefer digital payments. The security deposit can be paid via the same methods. All payments are secure and we provide official receipts.

What if I return the gadget late?

Late returns incur additional charges equivalent to one full day's rental fee for every 24-hour period (or fraction thereof). Please contact us immediately if you anticipate a late return so we can try to accommodate you and minimize additional charges.

Are the gadgets insured?

Our equipment has basic coverage, but we highly recommend our optional insurance for complete protection. The insurance covers accidental damage, theft, and loss. It's a small additional fee that can save you from potentially large replacement costs.

Can I use the gadgets abroad or outside Manila?

Yes, you can use our gadgets anywhere in the Philippines or abroad. However, you're responsible for the equipment throughout the rental period. For international use, we may require additional documentation and a higher security deposit. Please inform us of your travel plans when booking.
